# Changelog

## 0.12.2
- **Reply.** Say "Say in reply, ..." or "Reply, ..." to whisper back to whoever whispered you last. There's also a **Reply** channel in the D-Down list (it shows their name), and `/r` works from the keyboard.

## 0.12.1
- **Light trail for swipe typing.** A glowing streak that fades behind the ring. Choose Light, Dots or Off.
- **Trail colors:** Gold, Fel green, Frost blue, Arcane purple, Fire, Holy white, Blood red, or your class color. The ring matches.

## 0.12.0
- **Swipe typing** (Settings → Sticks → Swipe). One QWERTY keyboard and one ring; hold L2/R2, trace a word, let go.
  - Live guess over the ring while you trace; other guesses in the bar (D-Left/Right swaps them in).
  - Automatic spaces, sentence capitals, `I`, apostrophes (`dont` → `don't`), punctuation tucked against the word.
  - A quick press types a single key.
- **10,000-word English dictionary** for swipe and suggestions (from the wordfreq project, CC BY-SA 4.0).

## 0.11.x
- The talk button is claimed by Comunimate, so the PS5 mic no longer opens your bags.
- L1 + R1 + D-Down opens the full keyboard; holding the talk button opens the small "Listening to your wisdom" box with Send and Cancel buttons.
- Settings window banner.
- Mascot roster: 9 races × male/female × Cute/Epic/Anime, with parody emblems.

## 0.10.0
- General and Trade chat in the channel picker and by voice ("say in general chat ...").
- "Small box" is now the default when talking.
- Pick any controller button, or a keyboard key / mouse button, to hold to talk.

## 0.9.0
- Remap every keyboard button in Settings.
- Voice-only "Listening to your wisdom" box.
- "Say in party chat, ..." picks the channel by voice.

## 0.8.0
- Mascots for every race, male and female.
- Gold frame on/off.

## 0.7.0 and earlier
- Mascot styles (Cute / Epic / Anime), orc tray icon, spring / free / snap ring styles, settings window, WoW-style keyboard, secure chat sending, one-click installer with the voice helper in the system tray.
