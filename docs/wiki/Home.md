# Comunimate

**Chat in World of Warcraft: Forever with just a controller, or just your voice.**

Comunimate is two things that work together:

- **The add-on** (in game): a controller keyboard with WoW-aware word suggestions, swipe typing, a channel picker, a mascot that reacts when you talk, and a full settings window you can drive with the controller.
- **The helper** (a small Windows app in your system tray): hold a button, talk, and your words are typed for you. Speech recognition runs on your own PC.

## The 30-second version

1. Run `Comunimate-Setup.exe` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/ClobberSoft/Comunimate/releases).
2. Start WoW: Forever.
3. **L1 + R1 + D-Down** opens the keyboard. **Hold the mic button** to talk. **Click your mascot** for settings.
