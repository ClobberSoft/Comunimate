# Swipe Typing

Trace whole words with the stick, like swiping on a phone.

**Turn it on:** Settings → Sticks → Stick style → **Swipe**.

## How it works

The keyboard becomes one standard QWERTY block with **one ring**. **Either stick** moves it.

1. Put the ring on the word's first letter.
2. **Hold L2 or R2** and move through the word's letters. You don't need to stop on each one; just pass over them roughly in order. Turning at a letter helps.
3. **Let go.** The word is typed with a space after it.

While you trace, **the best guess floats above the ring** and the next guesses fill the bar.

## When it guesses wrong

- **D-Right / D-Left** swaps in the other guesses, in place.
- **Triangle** keeps the current word.
- **Square** deletes a letter at a time.

## Single keys

A **quick press without moving** types the key under the ring. Use it for names, `gg`, and the **`, . ?`** keys at the end of the bottom row. **R1** switches to numbers and symbols (taps only).

## Done for you

- A **space** after every word.
- Punctuation tucks against the word before it (`ready.`, not `ready .`).
- A **capital** at the start of each sentence, and `I` on its own.
- **Apostrophes:** swiping `dont` gives **don't**, `im` gives **I'm**, `youre` gives **you're**, and so on.

## Trail

Settings → Sticks:

- **Swipe trail:** **Light** (a glowing streak that fades behind the ring), **Dots**, or **Off**.
- **Trail color:** Gold, Fel green, Frost blue, Arcane purple, Fire, Holy white, Blood red, or **My class color**. The ring matches.

## Tips

- **Ring speed** (Settings → Sticks) matters a lot. Faster is easier for long words, slower for precision.
- Sharp turns are what the guesser listens to most. Turn clearly at each letter where the word changes direction.
- It knows about **10,000 common English words plus WoW terms** (LFM, OOM, classes, dungeons...) and **learns the words you send**, so it gets better the more you use it.
- Words it doesn't know (player names, rare slang) are easier to tap or to [say](Voice).

## How accurate is it?

Tested against simulated stick swipes with normal wobble: the right word comes first about 95% of the time and is in the bar 98–99% of the time. Real thumbs differ, so tell us which words it gets wrong ([open an issue](https://github.com/ClobberSoft/Comunimate/issues)).
