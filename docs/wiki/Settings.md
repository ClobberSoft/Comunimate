# Settings

Open with: **click your mascot**, the keyboard's **Settings** button, `/cm options`, or Options → AddOns → Comunimate.

Everything applies straight away. The window works with the mouse or the controller:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| D-Up / D-Down | Pick a setting |
| D-Left / D-Right | Change it (moves sliders too) |
| Cross | Press / flip |
| L1 / R1 | Previous / next page |
| Circle | Close |

## General
- **Controller:** PlayStation, Xbox or Steam button icons.
- **Send to:** the default channel (Auto, Party, Raid, Instance, Guild, Say, Yell, General, Trade, Reply).
- **Close after sending.**
- **Controller chat button opens the keyboard:** L1 + R1 + D-Down opens Comunimate instead of the normal chat box.

## Buttons / More buttons
Remap every keyboard action. Pick an action, press the controller button you want. If that button already did something, the two **swap**. **Reset buttons** puts everything back.

## Look
- **Keyboard size** and **Background opacity**.
- **Show keyboard:** preview while you adjust.
- **Reset position:** drag the keyboard with the mouse to move it; this puts it back.

## Mascot
- **Race:** My character, or any of Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead, Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, Skyborne.
- **Male or female:** Mine, Male, Female.
- **Style:** Cute, Epic, Anime.
- A live preview of the quiet and talking faces. See [Mascots](Mascots).

## Badge
- **Show the mascot**, **Size**, **Gold frame**.
- **Move the mascot:** turn on, drag it, turn off.
- **Reset position.**

## Sticks
- **Stick style:** Spring, Free, Snap, Swipe (see [Controls](Controls) and [Swipe Typing](Swipe-Typing)).
- **Swipe trail:** Light, Dots, Off. **Trail color:** eight presets or your class color.
- **Ring speed.**
- **Snap: reach for the edge keys:** how far to push in Snap style.
- **Flip up and down.**

## Voice
- **When you talk, show:** Small box or Keyboard.
- **Talk button (controller)** and **Talk key (keyboard or mouse)**; `/reload` after changing.
- **Talking opens Comunimate:** off = your words go into whatever text box is open.

## Words
- **Learn my words:** suggestions and swipe get better from what you send. Stays on your PC.
- **Forget learned words.**

## About
- Version, commands, and **Debug messages** for troubleshooting.
