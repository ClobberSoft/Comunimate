# Mascots

Your mascot sits on screen and reacts: **relaxed while you're quiet, shouting while you talk**. Click it to open the settings.

## Pick yours

Settings → **Mascot**:

- **Race:** Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead (Horde) · Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome (Alliance) · Skyborne (either side). **My character** follows whoever you're playing.
- **Male or female:** **Mine** follows your character.
- **Style:**
  - **Cute:** chibi cartoon, whistling with happy eyes, comically yelling when you talk.
  - **Epic:** painted heroic portrait, a battle cry when you talk.
  - **Anime:** anime portrait in front of the race's homeland.

That's 108 portraits in all (9 races × 2 × 3 styles × quiet/talking).

Settings → **Badge** controls the size, the gold frame, and moving it.

## Art

All mascot art is original artwork made for Comunimate. It uses parody emblems, never game logos or faction crests.

Want to make your own? See the [art guide](https://github.com/ClobberSoft/Comunimate/blob/main/art/ART_GUIDE.md) for the picture format and naming.
